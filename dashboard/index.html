Port Resilience Index

Method

Four components, each min–max scaled to 0–100 across ports and equally weighted:

  • Growth – compound annual growth of tonnage over the window.
  • Stability – standard deviation of annual log changes (lower = better).
  • Downside protection – maximum drawdown from a previous peak (shallower = better).
  • Cargo diversification – 1 − Herfindahl index of cargo-type shares in the latest year.

Robustness: the index is recomputed under 2,000 random weightings (Dirichlet). The rank range shows the 5th–95th percentile rank; the Spearman figure is the average rank correlation with the equal-weight result.

Only ports with a complete series and ≥1 million tonnes in the latest year are included. Malmö is excluded because Eurostat publishes no Malmö figures after 2020.
